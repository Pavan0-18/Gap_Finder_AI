SECTION A: CORE CONCEPTS

Question 1. [2 marks]
Identify the protein complex responsible for holding sister chromatids together from S phase through metaphase, and name the specific enzyme responsible for its cleavage at anaphase onset.

Question 2. [5 marks]
Describe the role of the spindle assembly checkpoint (SAC) during metaphase, and explain the cytological consequence if a cell enters anaphase with unattached kinetochores.

Question 3. [5 marks]
During Meiosis I, explain how mono-orientation of sister kinetochores and centromeric shugoshin protection ensure the segregation of homologous chromosomes rather than individual chromatids.

Question 4. [3 marks]
State three distinct mechanisms operative during sexual reproduction that contribute to genetic variation among offspring.
